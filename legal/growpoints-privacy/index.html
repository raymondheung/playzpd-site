Information we collect

Growpoints is designed to work without PlayZPD receiving personal information from the app. The current release does not require account creation or login.

The app stores progress data locally on your device so core features can work. This may include pet choice, pet progress, care points, habit names, tiny actions, check-in status and history, rescue, pause, archive, memory records, reminder preference, and similar in-app settings.

The current app also stores a local app user record and a device identifier from Android Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID in its on-device database. This is used to keep local app records connected to the same app installation. In the current release, the app does not send this identifier to PlayZPD servers.

If you email us for support or privacy questions, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include in that message.

Android system backup, restore, or device transfer may copy local app data depending on your device and Google account settings. Those features are provided by Android or Google, not by Growpoints.

How we use information

Growpoints uses local app data to operate core app features, save progress on your device, show care state and progress history, support local reminders when enabled, and improve your in-app experience.

We do not use app data from the current release for advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking you across apps or websites.

Data sharing

We do not sell user data.

In the current release, Growpoints does not share app-collected personal data with third parties. The source audit for this policy found no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash reporting SDK, billing integration, account system, cloud sync, or remote backend used by the app.

Local storage, retention, and deletion

Local app data remains on your device unless you clear Growpoints app data, uninstall the app, or use an operating-system feature such as device backup, restore, or transfer.

Because the current release does not create a server account, PlayZPD does not keep a server-side Growpoints account record for deletion. To delete local Growpoints data, use Android app settings to clear app data or uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

Growpoints is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy question about Growpoints, contact us at playzpdcom@gmail.com.

Security

Growpoints is designed to minimize data collection and keep progress data local on your device where applicable. No app can guarantee perfect security, but limiting collection reduces the amount of information at risk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Growpoints changes how it handles data, including if future versions add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, purchases, crash reporting, or other third-party services. When we update this page, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
