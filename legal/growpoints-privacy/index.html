Guest mode and local progress

Growpoints works without an account. Your pet choices, actions, check-ins, Care, progress, reports, pause or archive state, reminders, and pet media are stored on your device.

Growpoints does not upload that progress when you sign in. Android backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for Growpoints.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose Continue with Google, Growpoints uses Firebase Authentication. Firebase receives and stores a Firebase user ID and, when Google supplies them, your email address, display name, profile-photo URL, and authentication metadata.

Growpoints stores the same identity fields locally to show your account status. Signing in attaches that identity to the existing on-device profile; it does not upload, replace, or reset local pet, action, Care, check-in, or growth data.

Signing out removes the account binding from this device while leaving local progress available in guest mode.

Analytics

Growpoints uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand activation, feature use, and retention. The service automatically processes an app-instance identifier, a masked IP address used to derive approximate location, device and app information, screen and session activity, and app lifecycle events.

Growpoints also records a limited product funnel: onboarding completion, guest continuation, Google sign-in start, success, failure or cancellation, first Care, and completion of a Build or Reduce action.

These events do not include your Firebase user ID, email, name, photo, habit name, habit rule, Care total, or other free-form text. Advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals are disabled. Growpoints does not set an Analytics User-ID.

Services not used

This release does not use Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Storage, cloud progress synchronization, Firebase App Check, Crashlytics, advertising SDKs, payment SDKs, or AI services. Current pet media is bundled with the app or stored locally.

Use and sharing

Authentication data is used for sign-in and account management. Analytics data is used for product measurement and improvement. Google processes this data to provide Firebase Authentication and Google Analytics for Firebase under its applicable terms.

PlayZPD does not sell personal information and does not use the collected data for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you clear Growpoints storage or uninstall the app. Firebase Authentication keeps logged IP addresses for a few weeks and keeps other authentication information until the account is deleted. Google states that deleted Authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days.

In Growpoints, open Account and choose Delete account. The app deletes your Firebase Authentication identity and removes its local account binding. Local pet, action, Care, and progress data remains on the device as guest data unless you separately clear Growpoints storage or uninstall the app.

You can also request account deletion without the app through the public Growpoints account deletion page.

Your choices

You can use Growpoints as a guest, cancel Google sign-in, sign out, switch Google accounts, delete the connected account, or remove all local data through Android system settings. Google sign-in is not required for local check-ins.

Children's privacy

Growpoints is intended for users age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and have a privacy question, contact playzpdcom@gmail.com.

Security

Growpoints minimizes collection, keeps progress local, disables app backup, and uses encrypted connections for Firebase services. No app can guarantee perfect security.
