Account and Data Deletion

Delete a Growpoints account

Effective date: August 9, 2026

Delete in the app

  1. Open Growpoints.
  2. Open Habits, then Settings, then Account.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.
  4. If requested, complete the recent Google authentication prompt.

In-app deletion removes the Firebase Authentication identity and local account binding. It keeps pet, action, Care, check-in, and growth progress on this device as guest data.

Request deletion without the app

Email Growpoints support from the Google email connected to the account. Use the subject Growpoints account deletion request. Do not send passwords, identity documents, check-in details, or other sensitive information.

Contact: playzpdcom@gmail.com

Request account deletion

PlayZPD may ask for a minimal ownership-verification step before deleting the Firebase Authentication identity.

Data included

The request covers the Firebase Authentication identity used by Growpoints, including its Firebase user ID and available Google email, display name, profile-photo URL, and authentication metadata.

Growpoints does not store pet progress, actions, Care, or check-ins in Cloud Firestore or Firebase Cloud Storage.

Local data

To remove local pet, action, Care, check-in, and progress data as well, clear Growpoints storage in Android settings or uninstall Growpoints.

Growpoints Analytics events are not linked by Growpoints to your Firebase user ID or email. Account deletion does not make those pseudonymous measurement events identifiable.

Timing and retention

PlayZPD will process verified email requests within a reasonable period. Firebase states that deleted Authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is initiated.
