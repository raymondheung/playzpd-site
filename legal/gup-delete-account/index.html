Account and Data Deletion for GUP: Points Grow Pets

Effective date: August 10, 2026

In-app deletion Open Account and select Delete account.
Email request Send deletion requests to playzpdcom@gmail.com.
Local data remains Groups, Members, pets, and points remain available for Guest/local use.

How to request deletion

To delete the signed-in authentication account in GUP, open Account, select Delete account, and confirm Delete account. This deletes the Firebase Authentication account used for Google Sign-In or Email/Password.

If you cannot use the in-app option, request deletion by emailing PlayZPD at playzpdcom@gmail.com. Include the email address used for Google Sign-In or Email/Password. We suggest using this subject line:

GUP Account Deletion Request

PlayZPD may need to verify the request before deleting the authentication account.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and its authentication records, including the account's Firebase user identifier and sign-in access.

GUP's local Group names, Members, initials, optional Names and Nicknames, pets, points, progress, and settings are not stored in the authentication account. They remain on the device and are available for Guest/local use after authentication account deletion.

To remove that local data too, separately clear GUP's app data in Android settings or uninstall GUP.

Limited retention

Some records may be retained only if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, or Google Play compliance reasons.

If optional Google Play in-app purchases, subscriptions, upgrades, or paid digital features are added, Google Play may retain billing, subscription, refund, tax, or transaction records under Google Play policies and your Google account controls.
