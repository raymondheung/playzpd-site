Intended audience

GUP is intended for adults, educators, parents, guardians, coaches, and group leaders who use points and virtual pet progress to support groups, classes, habits, chores, routines, activities, or similar motivational systems.

GUP is not directed to children under 13. Children under 13 should not create accounts, use Google Sign-In, or submit personal information to GUP.

If you use GUP to track students, children, team members, or group members, you should use nicknames, initials, or non-identifying labels instead of full names or other identifying personal information.
